Privacy Policy for Puzzle Marbles

Last updated: October 2026

Puzzle Marbles ("we", "our", or "the App") is developed by Robert Yegon. We are committed to protecting and respecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our application collects, uses, and discloses information when you use our mobile game.

1. Information We Collect

a. Automatically Collected Information

Puzzle Marbles does not require an account or registration to play. However, third-party services integrated into the app may automatically collect certain device and usage information, including:

  • Device details (such as device manufacturer, model, operating system version, and system language).
  • Internet and network connectivity status.
  • IP address and approximate general location (country or city level).
  • Advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID / GAID) for ad delivery and measurement.
  • Diagnostic and performance data (such as crash logs and app responsiveness).

b. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Your gameplay progress (completed levels, earned stars, coins, high scores, power-ups, and audio/graphics preferences) is stored locally on your device. We do not store or transmit your personal game saves to private external servers.

2. How Information is Used

We and our service providers use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To operate, maintain, and provide the core features of the game.
  • To display banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements that help support free access to the game.
  • To monitor and analyze game stability, crash occurrences, and technical performance.
  • To verify active network connectivity required for online features and ads.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

Puzzle Marbles integrates third-party services that may collect information used to identify your device in accordance with their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services:
    https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob (Advertising):
    Google AdMob may serve advertisements based on your device identifier and general location. You can manage or reset your personalized advertising settings on Android via: Settings > Google > Ads > Reset advertising ID / Delete advertising ID.
    For more details, visit: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

4. Children’s Privacy

Puzzle Marbles is designed for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions). If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can take appropriate steps to delete such data.

5. Data Security

We value your trust in using our game. Although no method of electronic storage or internet transmission is 100% secure, we utilize standard industry practices and rely on Google's trusted developer infrastructure to safeguard your information.

6. Your Rights and Data Deletion

Because Puzzle Marbles does not create personal user accounts on external servers, you can reset all locally saved game data at any time by clearing the application storage in:

Settings > Apps > Puzzle Marbles > Storage > Clear Data.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. You are encouraged to review this page periodically for changes.
